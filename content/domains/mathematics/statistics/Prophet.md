# Prophet

Forecasting library for [[Time series]] with trend + seasonality.

- Good fit for business metrics: calendar effects, holidays, trend shifts, missing data.
- Workflow-style API: fit → make future dates → forecast with uncertainty intervals.
- Higher level than [[ARIMA]] or [[Exponential Smoothing]].
