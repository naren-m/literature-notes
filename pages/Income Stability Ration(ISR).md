# Income Stability Ration(ISR)

- How much corpus to retire without eating the principal, even in a bad market?
- Don't plan on linear 10-12% every year. Count the stable, low-volatility money that pays the monthly income.

$$\text{ISR} = \frac{\text{Stable Income Bucket} + \text{Low Volatility Bucket} + \text{Equity Bucket}}{\text{Annual Withdrawal Requirements}}$$

- Denominator = monthly income × 12. ₹1 lakh/month → ₹12 lakhs/year.
- 3 buckets
  - **Stable income** - safe debt, floater, short-term, arbitrage, "SWP safe" equity savings funds.
  - **Low volatility** - hybrid, conservative, balance advantage, low-volatility multi-asset.
  - **Equity** - long-term pure equity: flexicap, multicap.

| ISR | Zone |
|---|---|
| < 17.5 | High risk. Bad years break the withdrawals. Sequence of return risk is high. |
| 17.5 - 20 | Acceptable, if returns are normal and inflation is controlled. |
| > 24 | Comfortable. Don't touch equity even when the market falls. |

- Example: ₹12 lakhs/year × 17.5 = ₹2.1 crores.
  - "Squirreling Strategy" split: ₹70 lakhs stability + ₹70 lakhs income + ₹70 lakhs growth.
- 3-bucket discipline → no need for the "5 crore myth".
- ISR = stress test for a bridge. Withdrawals = daily traffic. Buckets = pillars. Market crash = earthquake.
