# Smrti (स्मृति)

anubhūta-viṣaya-asampramoṣaḥ smṛtiḥ - [[YogaSutras]] 1.11

Memory. An experienced object not slipping away.

- No new knowledge. Replays an old vṛtti.
- Last of the five vṛttis: [[Pramana]], viparyaya, vikalpa, [[nidra]], smṛti.
- Other meaning: smṛti = remembered texts, vs śruti = heard (Vedas).
