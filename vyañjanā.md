# vyañjanā

**Vyañjanā** is one of the three [[sabda-vrittis]]. It is the suggestive function of language: meaning that is not stated directly but is understood from context.

**3. व्यञ्जना — _vyañjanā_ — the suggestive / evocative function.** Beyond the denoted and the indicated, there remains a further layer that is _suggested_. Neither said directly nor substituted by convention — evoked. The delivered meaning is **vyaṅgyārtha** or simply **dhvani** ("resonance").
