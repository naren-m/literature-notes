# Charaka Samhita (चरकसंहिता)

Main text of [[Ayurvedam]]. Internal medicine.

- Lineage (from [[Suthrasthana]]):
  - [[Atreya]] taught six students.
  - [[Agnivesa]], the best of them, wrote the Agniveśa Tantra.
  - [[Charaka]] redacted it → this text.
  - Dṛḍhabala later completed missing chapters.
- 8 sthānas, 120 chapters. First is Sūtrasthāna.
- With Suśruta Saṃhitā and [[AshtangaHrydayam]]: the bṛhat-trayī, the three big Ayurveda texts.
