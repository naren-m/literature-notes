# Purushartha

Aims of human life = [[Dharma]], artha, kama, [[Moksha]].

- [[NirvanaShatakam]] names all four, then says: not me.
- Not denying them. Just - the Self is not defined by merit, wealth, desire, or even liberation as something to own.
- [[karma]]
