# Shri (श्री)

Mahālakṣmī. Consort of Viṣṇu.

- One who goes between: she takes the soul to the Lord and the Lord's grace to the soul (puruṣakāra).
- Dvaya mantra opens with her: śrīman nārāyaṇa caraṇau śaraṇaṃ prapadye.
- The name is read six ways from three roots (√śri, √śru, √śṝ): she is sought, she goes to the Lord, she hears, she makes Him hear, she removes obstacles, she ripens the soul. → [[Shri_Six_Derivations]]
- [[VishnuSahasranamam]] has a run of names built on śrī: 604 shreepatih, 607–614 shreeshah … shreemaan.
