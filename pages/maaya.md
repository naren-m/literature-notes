# Māyā

This research note records a Vedānta explanation of **māyā** as that which is different from both the existing and the non-existing.

The note also records the claim that māyā obscures knowledge of the self and connects the topic to [[advaita]]. The quoted Sanskrit and its source need verification.

## Source evidence

The original note linked this transcription to a timestamped video:

> [Maaya tu prakruthim vidhi, Mayinam thu maheswara](https://youtu.be/KWbuqC3xVD4?t=6131)

It also recorded the variant transcription **"māyāṃ tu prakuryāt vidhiḥ"** or **"māyāṃ tu prakaraṇaṃ vidhiḥ"**. The Sanskrit transcription and translation need verification against the source.
