# Dakshinayana (दक्षिणायन)

Six months when the sun moves south.

- Traditional start: Karka Saṅkrānti (mid-July).
- Astronomical start: summer solstice (about 21 June).
- Other half: [[Uthrayana]]. → [[jyothisha]]
