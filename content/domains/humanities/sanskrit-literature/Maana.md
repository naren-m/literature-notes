# Maana (मान)

Pride. Self-importance.

- [[Bhagavadgita]] 13.8 (13.7 in some editions) starts the qualities of knowledge with amānitvam: no pride. Then adambhitvam, [[Ahimsa]].
- Talked about in [[vedanta]].
