# Uthrayana (उत्तरायण)

Six months when the sun moves north.

- Traditional start: Makara Saṅkrānti (mid-January).
- Astronomical start: winter solstice (about 21 December). The traditional date has drifted from it (precession).
- Bhishma waited for uttarāyaṇa to leave his body.
- Other half: [[Dakshinayana]]. → [[jyothisha]]
