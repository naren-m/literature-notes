# Shatprashni (षट्प्रश्नी)

Six questions Yudhishthira asks Bhishma. The answer is [[VishnuSahasranamam]].

1. kim ekaṃ daivataṃ loke - who is the one God?
2. kiṃ vāpy ekaṃ parāyaṇam - the one final refuge?
3. stuvantaḥ kaṃ - praising whom,
4. kam arcantaḥ prāpnuyur mānavāḥ śubham - worshipping whom, do people reach the good?
5. ko dharmaḥ sarva-dharmāṇāṃ bhavataḥ paramo mataḥ - highest dharma, in your view?
6. kiṃ japan mucyate jantur janma-saṃsāra-bandhanāt - chanting what is one freed from birth and saṃsāra?

Answer: Vishnu, through his thousand names.
