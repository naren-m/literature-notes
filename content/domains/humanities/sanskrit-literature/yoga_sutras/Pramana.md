# Pramana (प्रमाण)

pratyakṣa-anumāna-āgamāḥ pramāṇāni - [[YogaSutras]] 1.7

Right knowledge of a real thing. First of the five vṛttis (YS 1.6).

- Three means of knowing:
  - [[Prathyaksha]] - see it directly
  - [[Anumaana]] - infer it
  - [[Aagama]] - trusted word
- The other four vṛttis: [[viparyaya]] (wrong knowledge), [[Vikalpa]] (only words), [[nidra]], [[smrthi]].
- Nyāya counts four pramāṇas: adds upamāna (comparison). [[NyayaSutra]]
