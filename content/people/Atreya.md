# Atreya (आत्रेय)

Punarvasu Ātreya. Taught Ayurveda. Did not write [[charaka_samhitha]]; it is his teaching, written down by his student.

- Received it through: Brahma → Prajapati → Ashwinis → Sahasraksha (Indra) → Bharadvaja → Atreya.
- Taught six students: Bhela, Jatukarna, Parashara, Harita, Ksharapani, [[Agnivesa]].
  - Each wrote a treatise and presented it to him. Agnivesa's was the best.
- Text chain: Atreya taught → [[Agnivesa]] wrote → [[Charaka]] redacted → Dṛḍhabala completed.
- His field: kāyacikitsā, internal medicine.
- Source: [[Suthrasthana]]
