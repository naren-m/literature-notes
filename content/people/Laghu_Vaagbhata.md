# Laghu Vaagbhata

The younger Vāgbhaṭa.

- One view: Vṛddha (elder) [[Vaagbhata]] wrote [[AstangaSangraha]]; Laghu Vāgbhaṭa wrote [[AshtangaHrydayam]].
- Other view: one author for both.
- Not settled. [[AshtangaHrydayam]] note has the same doubt.
