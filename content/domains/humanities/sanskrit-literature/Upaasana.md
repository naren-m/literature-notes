# Upaasana (उपासना)

upa + āsana = sitting near. Meditating on a chosen form until the mind rests there.

- [[Upanishads]] give many upāsanās: on Om, on prāṇa, on the sun.
- Order often given: karma → upāsanā → [[Gnanam]].
- Talked about in [[vedanta]].
