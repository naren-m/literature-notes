# Brihadesi

Wiki: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brihaddeshi

Early classical treatise on [[Carnatic music]] theory.

- Defines [[Nada]] (sound) and its nature.
- Early systematic rāga classification.
- Brihadesi → [[Sangitaratnakaram]] builds on it.
- Modern rāga system → [[Katapayadi_Melakarta_Guide]]
