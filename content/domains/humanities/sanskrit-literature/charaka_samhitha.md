# Charaka Samhita (चरकसंहिता)

Main text of [[Ayurvedam]]. Internal medicine (kāyacikitsā).

prayojanaṃ cāsya svasthasya svāsthya-rakṣaṇam āturasya vikāra-praśamanaṃ ca

Aim: keep the healthy healthy; cure the sick. (Sūtrasthāna 30.26)

## Lineage (from [[Suthrasthana]])

- [[Atreya]] taught six students.
- [[Agnivesa]], the best of them, wrote the Agniveśa Tantra.
- [[Charaka]] redacted it → this text.
- Dṛḍhabala later filled the gaps: 17 chapters of Cikitsā, and all of Kalpa and Siddhi.

## Structure: 8 sthānas, 120 chapters

| Sthāna | Chapters | About |
|---|---|---|
| Sūtra | 30 | principles, diet, daily routine |
| Nidāna | 8 | causes of disease |
| Vimāna | 8 | measures: diagnosis, doses, teaching |
| Śārīra | 8 | body, embryology |
| Indriya | 12 | signs of prognosis |
| Cikitsā | 30 | treatment |
| Kalpa | 12 | preparing medicines |
| Siddhi | 12 | purification therapies |

## Core ideas

- Tridoṣa: vāta, pitta, kapha. Health = balance.
- Trisūtra: hetu (cause), liṅga (signs), auṣadha (treatment).

## Place

- Bṛhat-trayī (the big three): Charaka Saṃhitā, Suśruta Saṃhitā (surgery), [[AshtangaHrydayam]].
- Date: not certain.
