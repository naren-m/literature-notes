# Mithya (मिथ्या)

Neither real nor unreal. Appears, then gets cancelled.

- Rope-snake: you saw it, so not unreal. It goes when you see the rope, so not real.
- viparyayo mithyā-jñānam atad-rūpa-pratiṣṭham - [[YogaSutras]] 1.8 → [[viparyaya]]
- [[advaita]]: the world is mithya. Sad-asad-vilakṣaṇa → [[maaya]]
