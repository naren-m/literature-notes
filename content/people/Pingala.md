# Pingala (पिङ्गल)

Author of the Chandaḥśāstra, on Sanskrit prosody (meter).

- Meters are patterns of short (laghu) and long (guru) syllables. Counting and listing them gives binary-like enumeration and the meru-prastāra, the same triangle as Pascal's.
- The Fibonacci-type count is implied in this work. Stated explicitly later: Virahāṅka, then Hemachandra → [[Hemachandra numbers]], [[fibbonacci numbers]].
- Date: not certain. Usually placed in the last centuries BCE.
