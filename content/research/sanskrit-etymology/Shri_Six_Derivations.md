# Śrī: six derivations (Pāñcarātra)

The Pāñcarātra Āgama reads the name [[Shri]] (Mahālakṣmī) six ways: śrīyate, śrayate, śṛṇoti, śrāvayati, śṛṇāti, śrīṇāti.

Status: research. The Sanskrit verse that lists all six has not been found yet. See open questions.

## The six derivations

Roots: √śri (sevāyām, to resort to), √śru (śravaṇe, to hear), √śṝ (hiṃsāyām, to destroy / wear away). → [[Dhatupatha]]

1. **śrīyate**: she is resorted to. All beings approach her for refuge.
2. **śrayate**: she resorts to the Lord. She goes to Nārāyaṇa on the soul's behalf.
3. **śṛṇoti**: she hears the cries of those who come to her.
4. **śrāvayati**: she makes the Lord hear those pleas.
5. **śṛṇāti**: she destroys obstacles, the soul's sins and karma.
6. **śrīṇāti**: she ripens / nourishes. Her qualities (compassion first) make the soul fit to serve the Lord.

## What the tradition builds on it

- Together the six describe her **puruṣakāra** role: mediator between jīva and Īśvara. In the Dvaya mantra (śrīman nārāyaṇa caraṇau śaraṇaṃ prapadye), "Śrī" names the one who leads the devotee to the Lord, pleads for them, and secures deliverance.
- **Vedānta Deśika, Rahasyatrayasāra, Dvaya chapter ("The meaning of Śrī")**: says Śrī is etymologically explained six ways and credits the derivations to the Bhagavat-śāstra (Pāñcarātra).
  - śrīyate / śrayate: resorted to by those seeking spiritual rebirth; she resorts to the Lord to grant it, softening His anger at offenders.
  - śṛṇoti / śrāvayati: hears the refugee, pleads for them (cites Varāha Purāṇa, Rāmāyaṇa [[Sundarakanda]]).
  - śṛṇāti: removes karma for those who did prapatti. Cites Lakṣmī Tantra 28.14, Itihāsa Samuccaya 10.51, Tiruvāymoḻi 4.5.11.
  - śrīṇāti: ripens through compassion. Cites Āḷavandār's Catuḥślokī 1, Bhaṭṭar's Śrī Guṇaratnakośa, Tiruvāymoḻi 9.2.1.
- **Piḷḷai Lokācārya, Śrīvacana Bhūṣaṇam sūtra 11**: uses the √śru pair. She corrects both Lord and jīva, so both śṛṇoti and śrāvayati apply.

## Pāñcarātra context

- **Lakṣmī Tantra**: the Pāñcarātra text devoted to Lakṣmī. Lakṣmī herself gives the upadeśa. Ch. 50 explains the Puruṣa and Śrī Sūktas. Puts Śrī at par with Viṣṇu.
- **Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā**: Viṣṇu and his Śakti inseparable, but not equal.
