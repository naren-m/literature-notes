# Authorization

What are you allowed to do?

- Comes after [[Authentication]] (who are you).
- Broken authorization → [[Elevation of Privilege]], the E in [[STRIDE]].
- Least privilege: grant only what the job needs.
- [[Zero-Trust]]: check on every request, not once at the door.
