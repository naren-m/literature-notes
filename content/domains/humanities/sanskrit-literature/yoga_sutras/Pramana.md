# Pramana (प्रमाण)

pratyakṣa-anumāna-āgamāḥ pramāṇāni - [[YogaSutras]] 1.7

Means of right knowledge. How you know what you know.

- pra + mā = measure out. The four words:
  - pramā = right knowledge
  - pramātā = the knower
  - prameya = what is known
  - pramāṇa = the means
- First of the five vṛttis (YS 1.6). Others: [[viparyaya]] (wrong knowledge), [[Vikalpa]] (only words), [[nidra]], [[smrthi]].
- Right knowledge is still a vṛtti. yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ (YS 1.2) stills this too, not only the wrong ones.

## The three in Yoga

| Means | What it gives | Example | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Prathyaksha]] | direct perception | see fire | only what is in front of the senses now |
| [[Anumaana]] | inference from a sign | smoke on the hill → fire | needs a link seen before |
| [[Aagama]] | trusted word | scripture, reliable person | only as good as the source |

## How many pramāṇas, by school

| School | Count | Accepts |
|---|---|---|
| Cārvāka | 1 | perception |
| Vaiśeṣika | 2 | + inference |
| Sāṅkhya, Yoga | 3 | + word (āgama / śabda) |
| Nyāya | 4 | + upamāna (comparison) → [[NyayaSutra]] |
| Mīmāṃsā (Prabhākara) | 5 | + arthāpatti (postulation) |
| Mīmāṃsā (Bhāṭṭa), Advaita | 6 | + anupalabdhi (non-perception) |
