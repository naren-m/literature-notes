# Advaita (अद्वैत)

Not two. Brahman alone is real.

- Jīva = Brahman. The difference is ignorance.
- World = [[maaya]]. [[Mithya]]: appears, but not ultimately real.
- Rope seen as snake: the snake was never there. Knowing the rope removes it. → [[Gnanam]]
- [[AdiShankaraCharya]] set it out through the three sources: [[Upanishads]], Brahma Sūtra ([[Brahma Sutra Bhasya]]), [[Bhagavadgita]].
- Talked about in [[vedanta]].
