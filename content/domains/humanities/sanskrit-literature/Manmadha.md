# Manmadha (मन्मथ)

God of desire. Personification of [[kaama]].

- manmatha = one who churns the mind.
- Bow of sugarcane, arrows of flowers.
- tuNTa viNTi vAni = the one with the sugarcane bow, in [[Bantu reethi Kolu]].
