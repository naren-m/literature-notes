# Kanaada (कणाद)

Sage. Founder of Vaiśeṣika.

- Wrote the [[VaishshikaSutra]] → [[Vaiseshika Darshanam]]
- Everything is built from paramāṇu: smallest particles that can't be split.
- kaṇa = particle, grain.
- Date: unknown.
