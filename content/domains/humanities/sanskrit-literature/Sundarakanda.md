# Sundarakanda (सुन्दरकाण्ड)

Fifth kāṇḍa of Vālmīki [[ramayanam]]. Hanuman's book.

- Crosses the ocean, finds Sita in Lanka, gives Rama's ring, burns Lanka, returns with Sita's message.
- Sarga 1 = sāgara laṅghanam, the leap. [[dhRti-dRShTi-mati-dAKShyam sloka]] is from there.
