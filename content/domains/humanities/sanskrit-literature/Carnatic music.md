# Carnatic music

South Indian classical music. Rāga (melody) + tāla (rhythm cycle).

- 72 melakartas = parent scales. Every rāga comes from one. → [[Katapayadi_Melakarta_Guide]]
- Trinity: [[tyagaraja swamy]], Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri.
- Older theory: [[Brihadesi]], [[Sangitaratnakaram]], [[Nada]]
