# Agnivesa (अग्निवेश)

Best student of [[Atreya]]. Wrote the Agniveśa Tantra, base of [[charaka_samhitha]].

- Fellow students: Bhela, Jatukarna, Parashara, Harita, Ksharapani.
- [[Charaka]] later redacted his text.
- Source: [[Suthrasthana]]
