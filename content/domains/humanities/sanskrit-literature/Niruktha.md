# Niruktha

*Nirukta* is the study of word meaning through explanation and etymology. It is one of the six Vedāṅgas, the auxiliary disciplines used to understand the Vedas.

The surviving work is associated with Yāska. It explains difficult Vedic words by examining roots, prefixes, suffixes, compounds, sound changes, and context. It also groups words into nouns, verbs, prefixes, and particles.

Nirukta is not just a list of translations. It records how a word can be interpreted and how its meaning relates to its form. [[Amarakosham]] complements it by organizing synonyms, while [[Ashtadhyayi]] focuses on grammatical structure.
