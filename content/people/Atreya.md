# Atreya (आत्रेय)

Punarvasu Ātreya. Teacher in [[charaka_samhitha]]. The text is his teaching.

- Six students; each wrote a treatise and presented it to him. Best: [[Agnivesa]].
- [[Suthrasthana]] note says "Atreya wrote charaka_samhitha". Usual account: Atreya taught, Agnivesa wrote, Charaka redacted. Check the source.
