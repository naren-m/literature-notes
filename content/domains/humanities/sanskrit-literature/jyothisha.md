# Jyothisha (ज्योतिष)

One of the six vedāṅgas. Time-keeping for ritual: sun, moon, nakṣatras.

- Called the eye of the Veda.
- Astronomy first. Astrology came later.
- Daily use: [[Panchangam]], [[tithi]]
- Text: [[SuryaSidhantam]]
- Sun's two halves: [[Uthrayana]], [[Dakshinayana]]
