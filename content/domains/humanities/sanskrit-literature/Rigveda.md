# Rigveda (ऋग्वेद)

Oldest of the four Vedas. Hymns (ṛc).

- 10 maṇḍalas, about 1028 sūktas.
- Hymns to [[Agni]], [[indra]], [[Soma]]. The whole 9th maṇḍala is Soma.
- Rishi Atri observing a solar eclipse is in the Rigveda (5.40) → [[jyothisha_rajvedam_beerbiceps]]
- Other Vedas: [[Atharvanaveda]]
