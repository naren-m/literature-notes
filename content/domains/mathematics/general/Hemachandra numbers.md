# Hemachandra numbers

1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ... Each number = sum of the previous two.

- Hemachandra, Jain scholar, Chandonuśāsana, about 1150 CE.
- Question he answered: how many rhythms fill n mātrās (beats), using 1-beat and 2-beat syllables?
  - Last syllable is 1 beat → rhythms of n-1. Last is 2 beats → rhythms of n-2. Add them.
- About 50 years before Fibonacci's Liber Abaci (1202). → [[fibbonacci numbers]]
- Older roots: [[Pingala]].
