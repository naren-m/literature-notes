# HowToSpeakLikeHanuma

Rama's description of Hanuman's speech presents effective communication as a combination of knowledge, precision, and controlled delivery.

The speech is described as:

grounded in knowledge of the Vedas and grammar;

free from mispronunciation;

concise rather than unnecessarily elaborate;

clear and unambiguous;

delivered neither too fast nor too slowly; and

supported by a pleasant, steady tone and appropriate physical expression.

The practical lesson is simple: understand the subject, choose clear words, respect the listener, and control pace and tone. A message can be correct and still fail if its delivery is confusing or poorly timed.

The source is [Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Kiṣkindhā Kāṇḍa, Sarga 3, verses 25–34](https://www.valmiki.iitk.ac.in/sloka?field_kanda_tid=4&language=dv&field_sarga_value=3).
