# Agastya (అగస్త్య / अगस्त्य)

अगं स्तम्भयति = one who stops the mountain. Aga = Vindhya. Also the star Canopus.

Spelling: अ-ग-स्-त्-य, unaspirated t. "Agasthya" = South Indian romanization.

Needs: [[Nan samasa]] · [[Dhatupatha]] · [[Krt pratyaya]] · [[Upapada samasa]]

## aga = what does not go

- na + ga (√gam, to go) = does not go → mountain, tree. [[Nan samasa]]
- 6.3.73 नलोपो नञः → n drops → a-ga.
- 6.3.77 नगोऽप्राणिष्वन्यतरस्याम् → non-living: naga or aga. Living: only aga.
  - अगो वृषलः शीतेन = a man frozen still by cold.

## aga + √styai → agastya

√स्त्यै शब्दसङ्घातयोः (DP 01.1058) = to sound; to become dense, still. [[Dhatupatha]]

1. agam + styai
2. → styā (6.1.45 आदेच उपदेशेऽशिति: ai → ā)
3. → styā + ka (3.2.3 आतोऽनुपसर्गे कः) [[Krt pratyaya]]
4. → stya (6.4.64 आतो लोप इटि च)
5. → upapada compound (2.2.19 उपपदमतिङ्) [[Upapada samasa]]
6. → **अगस्त्यः**

Same shape: पार्ष्णि + त्रै → पार्ष्णित्रम् (heel-guard).

## Other derivations (Apte, not checked)

- Agasti = aga + √as (to hurl) + ti → one who throws down the mountain.
  - Gap: needs the śakandhvādi vārttika; Apte's Uṇādi 4.179 doesn't match.
- aga = kumbha (pitcher) → kumbhasambhava, the pitcher-born.

## Pāṇini names him

- 2.4.70 आगस्त्यकौण्डिन्ययोरगस्तिकुण्डिनच् → plural stem = Agasti: आगस्त्यः / आगस्त्यौ / अगस्तयः.
- 6.4.149 सूर्यतिष्यागस्त्यमत्स्यानां य उपधायाः → आगस्ती = belonging to Agastya. The south is Agastya's direction.

## Amarakośa 1.3.215–216

agastyaḥ kumbhasambhavaḥ … maitrāvaruṇiḥ … lopāmudrā sadharmiṇī

- Agastya = Kumbhasambhava = Maitrāvaruṇi (son of Mitra + Varuṇa).
- Wife: Lopāmudrā.

## Open

- The Vindhya story is not in the Ṛgveda, where his hymns are (1.165–191). The name is older than the myth that explains it. The styai + ka derivation is clean grammar, not history.
- The Kāśikā examples are not checked against a source file.
