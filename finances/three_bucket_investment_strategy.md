# three bucket investment strategy

The three-bucket strategy separates money by when it will be needed. Its purpose is to reduce the chance that long-term investments must be sold during a market fall to fund short-term expenses.

## The buckets

| Bucket | Time horizon | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Stability | 0–3 years | fund near-term expenses with low-volatility assets |
| 2. Income | 3–7 years | refill Bucket 1 with moderate risk |
| 3. Growth | 10+ years | pursue long-term growth and help refill Bucket 2 |

The exact assets and percentages depend on the goal, cash-flow need, time horizon, tax treatment, and risk tolerance. The original note's return assumptions and ISR multipliers are planning inputs, not guarantees.

## How it works

Withdraw from Bucket 1. Refill it from Bucket 2 when the plan allows. Refill Bucket 2 from Bucket 3 during favorable periods instead of selling growth assets after a fall.

The model addresses **sequence-of-returns risk**: the order of gains and losses matters when withdrawals happen at the same time as market volatility.

This is a planning framework, not personal investment advice. Validate the asset choices, taxes, inflation assumptions, and withdrawal rate with a qualified adviser before acting.
