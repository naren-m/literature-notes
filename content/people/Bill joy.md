# Bill joy

- Bill Joy built Vi?
- Cofounded Sun Microsystems

American computer engineer and venture capitalist. Co-founded Sun Microsystems in 1982 with Scott McNealy, Vinod Khosla, and Andy Bechtolsheim. Chief Scientist and CTO there until 2003.
