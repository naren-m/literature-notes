# Anumaana (अनुमान)

Inference. From a sign to what it proves.

- anu + māna = measure after.
- Smoke on the hill → fire on the hill.
- Needs the link seen before: wherever smoke, there fire. That comes from [[Prathyaksha]].
- Second of the three [[Pramana]]s.
