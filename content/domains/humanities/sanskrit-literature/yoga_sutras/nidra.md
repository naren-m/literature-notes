# Nidra (निद्रा)

abhāva-pratyaya-ālambanā vṛttir nidrā - [[YogaSutras]] 1.10

Sleep is a vṛtti too. Mind holding on to absence.

- Wake up: "slept well, knew nothing". That memory means something was there to experience it.
- Fourth of the five vṛttis. Next: [[smrthi]].
