# Soma (सोम)

Vedic ritual drink, and a deity.

- Whole 9th maṇḍala of [[Rigveda]] is to Soma.
- Pressed, filtered, offered, drunk by priests. Linked with [[indra]].
- Which plant: unknown. Many theories, none settled.
- Later, Soma = the moon.
- [[Havan]]
