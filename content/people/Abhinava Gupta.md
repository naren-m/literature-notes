# Abhinava Gupta

Abhinavagupta. Kashmiri philosopher, around 10th–11th century.

- Kashmir Shaivism (Trika). Main work: Tantrāloka.
- Aesthetics: Abhinavabhāratī, his commentary on the Nāṭyaśāstra. Rasa theory.
