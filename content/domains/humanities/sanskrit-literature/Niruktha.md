# Niruktha

Nirukta = explanation. Why a word means what it means.

- One of the 6 Vedāṅgas. Yāska.
- Breaks hard Vedic words into root + prefix + suffix + context.
- 4 kinds of words: Nāma (noun), Ākhyāta (verb), Upasarga (prefix), Nipāta (particle).
- धर्म ← धृ (to hold) → [[Dharma]] = that which holds.
- Meaning = Nirukta. Form = [[Ashtadhyayi]]. Synonyms = [[Amarakosham]].
