# Gnanam (ज्ञानम्)

Knowledge. Knowing.

- Wrong knowledge = [[Mithya]] gnanam = [[viparyaya]].
- In Vedanta: knowing the Self. The last step after karma and [[Upaasana]].
- [[advaita]]: only gnanam removes ignorance. Action can't.
