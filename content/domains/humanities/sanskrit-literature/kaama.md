# Kaama (काम)

Desire.

- One of the four [[Purushartha]]s: dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa. Fine when it stays inside dharma.
- First of the six inner enemies (ariṣaḍvarga): kāma, krodha, lobha, moha, [[mada]], mātsarya.
- As a god: [[Manmadha]].
- In [[Bantu reethi Kolu]], Hanuman knocks kāma, [[mada]] and the rest to the ground.
