# kavi

In Sanskrit, **kavi** can mean a poet, sage, seer, or wise person. The word emphasizes insight and understanding, not only the production of verse.

The Rig Vedic prayer to Ganesha calls him **kaviṃ kaviṇām**, “the kavi among kavis.” In that setting, the word presents him as the foremost seer or wise speaker.

Modern usage often narrows *kavi* to “poet.” The broader meaning should be preserved when reading older Sanskrit texts.
