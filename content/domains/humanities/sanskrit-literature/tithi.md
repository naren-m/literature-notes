# tithi

Tithi = lunar day. Every 12° the Moon pulls ahead of the Sun = one tithi.

360° / 12° = 30 tithis in a lunar month.

~~~
angular distance = (lunar longitude - solar longitude) modulo 360
tithi = floor(angular distance / 12) + 1
~~~

- 1–15 → śukla pakṣa (bright half)
- 16–30 → [[krishna]] pakṣa (dark half). Subtract 15.
- Fraction left over → how much of the tithi has passed.
- Ekadasi etc. are tithis - that's why devotees care.

Nakshatra = (sidereal lunar longitude × 60) / 800. Pada = fractional part × 800 → 0–200 = 1st, 200–400 = 2nd, 400–600 = 3rd, 600–800 = 4th.

## Worked example (18 March 2023, 3:30 PM)

- Sun 333.364°, Moon 287.858°
- (287.858 - 333.364 + 360) / 12 = 26.207 → Krishna paksha, 26.207 - 15 = 11.207 → 12th = Dwadashi
- Nakshatra = 287.858 × 60 / 800 = 21.589 → 22nd = Sharvana. 0.589 × 800 = 471.2 → 3rd pada

Real panchangam also needs local time and sunrise rules. Ekadasi was till 11:16 AM, so the day is still Ekadashi.
