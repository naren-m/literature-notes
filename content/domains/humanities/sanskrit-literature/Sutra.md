# Sutra

A **sūtra** is a short rule or teaching written to preserve a large idea in very few words. Its compact form supports memorization and leaves room for explanation through commentary.

Traditional descriptions emphasize brevity, clarity, useful meaning, lack of needless repetition, and freedom from obvious error. A sūtra is therefore not merely a short sentence; it is a compact rule whose full meaning is developed by its surrounding tradition.

Examples include the [[YogaSutras]], [[NyayaSutra]], [[VaishshikaSutra]], and [[Sulba Sutras]]. The [[SivaSurta]] is another example of a compact rule tradition.
