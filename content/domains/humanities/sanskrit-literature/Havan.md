# Havan

Fire ritual. Offerings into [[Agni]] with mantras. Same idea as homa, yajña.

- Offerings: ghee, grains, samidh (sticks).
- Question from [[RanveerShow-Indain Archaeologist]]: "Havan is Soma?" [[Soma]] was one offering in Vedic yajña, not the ritual itself. Open: what the guest meant.
