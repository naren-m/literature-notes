# Ahimsa (अहिंसा)

Non-harming. In thought, word and act.

- First yama in [[YogaSutras]] 2.30.
- In the [[Bhagavadgita]]'s lists of qualities: 13.8 (amānitvam adambhitvam ahiṃsā ...) and 16.2.
- Talked about in [[vedanta]], with [[Maana]].
